Net metering & exports

net-metering NEM 2.0 keeps annual netting but cuts year-end exports to 5.829¢

  • Export compensation: Monthly net metering credits continue at TOU-equivalent rates; energy left over after the one-year netting period is settled at a fixed 5.829¢/kWh.
  • Export rate: 5.829¢/kWh — A $1.00/month administrative charge applies to NEM accounts. Residential systems are capped at 25 kW and 150% of historical annual use (Code §56-594).
  • Grandfathering: The SCC's April 30, 2026 Final Order (Case PUR-2025-00079) keeps energy-based annual netting for existing customers; the 5.829¢ year-end export rate and other NEM 2.0 terms apply to new or modified interconnections from the first of the month at least 12 months after the order (May 2027).
  • What's ahead: The statute itself (Code §56-594) has a successor version effective January 1, 2027. If you interconnect or modify your system in 2027, you will be on NEM 2.0 terms — model exports at 5.829¢, not retail.

Battery incentives in Virginia

Federal 30% credit: ended The 30% Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) ended for systems placed in service after Dec 31, 2025 (OBBBA). A cash/loan battery installed in 2026 gets $0 federal credit. Leases/PPAs may still earn a 30% credit under Section 48E, but it goes to the system owner, not you. State rebate
No state battery rebate

Virginia has no statewide battery rebate. HB1133 (a $50M solar + storage tax-credit program) was continued to the 2027 session and is not law.

What a battery is worth in Virginia

Strategy: Hybrid

Rates near the national average but rising, a four-hour-ish evening peak under the Off-Peak Plan, and year-end exports worth only 5.829¢ all point the same way: use the battery to shift load and cover outages rather than to sell power.

Cost context A typical 13.5 kWh system runs $10,000–$16,500 installed before incentives (national range).
Watch out The $1.588B rate increase is already flowing through bills (2026–2028), which improves the case for load shifting — but do not underwrite export revenue at retail, and confirm your interconnection date before assuming which NEM rules apply.
