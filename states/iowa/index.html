Net metering & exports

net-billing Retail-rate export credits now; Value of Solar is possible after July 2027, not automatic

  • Export compensation: Net billing (kWh credits) or inflow-outflow (dollar credits at the retail outflow rate), depending on your utility.
  • Export rate: Credits follow your utility's tariff. Net billing: excess kWh at the 12-month cash-out are paid at avoided cost, split evenly between you and the utility's low-income program. Inflow-outflow: unused dollar credits are forfeited at the annual cutoff (you choose a January or April date at interconnection).
  • Grandfathering: Existing facilities keep the net billing or inflow-outflow tariff in place at installation for 20 years. For inflow-outflow customers, the retail outflow purchase rate is fixed for 20 years; later VOS adoption or a change of ownership does not change it. There is no 2025 cutoff.
  • What's ahead: The IUC must start a Value of Solar proceeding if a utility petitions after July 1, 2027, or when statewide DG penetration reaches 5%, whichever is earlier. Statewide penetration was 3.90% in 2025 (IPL 8.65%, MidAmerican 1.47%), so the 5% trigger is not met.

Battery incentives in Iowa

Federal 30% credit: ended The 30% Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) ended for systems placed in service after Dec 31, 2025 (OBBBA). A cash/loan battery installed in 2026 gets $0 federal credit. Leases/PPAs may still earn a 30% credit under Section 48E, but it goes to the system owner, not you. IRS — Residential Clean Energy Credit ↗
State rebate
No state battery rebate

Iowa has no state battery rebate. Alliant and MidAmerican offer efficiency rebates (HVAC, insulation) but no battery-specific incentive.

What a battery is worth in Iowa

Strategy: Backup-first

Iowa's low rates and modest TOU spread mean pure arbitrage is thin for most customers. Winter storms and multi-day outages are the main driver. Alliant time-of-day customers can shift load, but MidAmerican's residential TOU is not published.

Cost context A typical 13.5 kWh system runs $10,000–$16,500 installed before incentives (national range).
Watch out A Value of Solar proceeding is possible after July 2027, but it is not automatic; existing systems keep their 20-year tariff terms. A VOS change would affect new interconnections, not the backup case.

What's changing in Iowa

Policy, rates and utility news that affect home batteries. Updated 2026-11-20.

What's coming

  • Jul 1, 2027
    Policy medium Upcoming

    Value of Solar petition window opens

    After July 1, 2027, an Iowa utility can petition the IUC to start a Value of Solar proceeding. The statewide 5% DG penetration trigger has not been reached.

    Why it matters: If a VOS proceeding starts, export credits for new interconnections could be lower. Existing systems keep their 20-year tariff terms.

    Iowa Code §476.49 ↗

What changed

  • Oct 28, 2026
    Utility medium

    DOE $5.1B loan package backs Alliant reliability plan

    Alliant Energy announced a $5.1 billion loan package from the U.S. Department of Energy that it projects could deliver up to $1.2 billion in customer savings, supporting natural gas plants and roughly 1,000 MW of battery storage.

    Why it matters: Accelerated storage and grid investment could shape Iowa's rate structure and battery participation over the long term.

    Alliant Energy — news ↗
  • Oct 26, 2026
    Event medium

    Iowa City outage affects 12,000 customers

    A power outage in Iowa City affected about 12,000 customers on October 26; roughly 10,000 were restored by noon.

    Why it matters: A regional outage event — another data point for the backup-insurance case in Iowa.

    KCRG — Iowa City power outage ↗
  • Sep 3, 2026
    Utility low

    Alliant Rock County LNG facility wins regulatory approval

    Alliant Energy's Rock County liquefied natural gas facility moved forward with regulatory approval.

    Why it matters: LNG supply supports winter gas reliability, which in turn affects heating costs and the winter-outage backup case.

    Alliant Energy — news ↗
  • Jul 8, 2026
    Rates medium

    MidAmerican files a gas rate increase request

    MidAmerican asked the IUC for a proposed natural gas rate increase; four consumer comment meetings were scheduled and interim rates took effect July 17.

    Why it matters: If approved, higher gas costs can flow through to electric bills — re-run your ROI with the new rate.

    Iowa Utilities Commission — news ↗
  • Jul 4, 2026
    Event medium

    MidAmerican storms leave thousands without power

    Overnight storms left more than 14,600 MidAmerican Energy customers without power at the July 4 peak (over 7,500 still out in the morning); a second August 12 storm left about 2,782 Iowa customers without power.

    Why it matters: Summer storm outages reinforce that home battery backup has real value in Iowa, beyond the winter outage case.

    WHO13 — MidAmerican storm outages ↗
  • Jan 13, 2026
    Utility medium

    Alliant Wever BESS (100 MW) and Wood County BESS come online

    Alliant Energy announced two battery storage systems fully operational: the 100 MW Wever BESS in Lee County, Iowa, and the 75 MW Wood County BESS in Wisconsin — 700 MWh combined, 4-hour, built with FlexGen and paired with adjacent solar fields.

    Why it matters: Iowa's first utility-scale battery storage marks the start of scaled battery storage in the state; Alliant plans roughly 400 MW of Iowa battery storage across these and other projects.

    Alliant Energy — news ↗
  • 2025 data (published 2026)
    Market medium

    Statewide DG penetration at 3.90% — below the 5% VOS trigger

    The IUC reports 2025 statewide distributed generation penetration of 3.90% (IPL 8.65%, MidAmerican 1.47%). The statutory trigger is a statewide 5%, not any single utility's number.

    Why it matters: The Value of Solar trigger is not met; the earliest a utility can petition for VOS remains July 1, 2027.

    Iowa Utilities Commission — on-site distributed generation ↗
  • Dec 31, 2025
    Policy high

    Federal 25D battery credit ends

    The 30% Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) ended for systems placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.

    Why it matters: Cash and loan purchases in 2026 get no federal credit — Iowa also has no state rebate, so the incentive stack is now utility programs only.

    IRS — Residential Clean Energy Credit ↗
  • Mar 12, 2020
    Policy medium

    SF 583 sets the net billing / inflow-outflow framework

    Iowa codified net billing and inflow-outflow tariffs for IPL and MidAmerican, with 20-year terms and a conditional path to Value of Solar.

    Why it matters: The 20-year term is why existing Iowa systems keep their export rate even if a VOS proceeding starts later.

    Iowa Legislature — SF 583 ↗
