Net metering & exports

vder VDER pays for exports at a location-based value — check capacity before counting on it

  • Export compensation: VDER (Value of Distributed Energy Resources): a location- and time-based value stack that varies by utility, system size and time of day.
  • Export rate: Published VDER rates differ widely by utility and have generally been well below retail. Con Edison's distributed-generation capacity has been heavily subscribed in recent years — confirm availability with your utility before assuming any export revenue.
  • Grandfathering: VDER compensation terms are set by your utility's program at interconnection; contract terms vary, so confirm what your interconnection agreement locks in.
  • What's ahead: New York continues to review distributed-generation compensation. Check NY DPS filings and your utility's tariff page for the latest VDER values and available capacity.

Battery incentives in New York

Federal 30% credit: ended The 30% Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) ended for systems placed in service after Dec 31, 2025 (OBBBA). A cash/loan battery installed in 2026 gets $0 federal credit. Leases/PPAs may still earn a 30% credit under Section 48E, but it goes to the system owner, not you. IRS — Residential Clean Energy Credit ↗
State rebate
NYSERDA pays $250/kWh (Con Ed) or $200/kWh (rest of state)

The NYSERDA Residential Energy Storage incentive pays $250/kWh in Con Edison territory and $200/kWh elsewhere, capped at 25 kWh per home — about $3,375 or $2,700 on a 13.5 kWh system. Systems must be new, permanent and installed by an approved contractor.

≈ $3,375 on a typical 13.5 kWh system.

What a battery is worth in New York

Strategy: Self-consumption

At 28.7¢ on average — and far higher in Con Ed territory — every kWh you avoid buying is worth more than in most states. The NYSERDA rebate covers roughly a quarter of a 13.5 kWh system, and a battery that covers evening peaks plus outages stacks both values.

Cost context A typical 13.5 kWh system runs $10,000–$16,500 installed before incentives (national range).
Watch out Export revenue is uncertain: VDER values vary by location and capacity has been tight. Do not underwrite the payback on exports — underwrite it on bill savings and the NYSERDA rebate.
